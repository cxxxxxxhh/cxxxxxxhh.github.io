SSRN WORKING PAPER2026

Simulation of Stochastic Volatility Models via Operator Splitting Schemes

Lilian Hu, Congxin He, Yue Kuen Kwok, Gongqiu Zhang

We propose an efficient simulation framework for stochastic volatility models based on Strang operator splitting. It avoids evaluating integrated variance conditional on terminal variance, applies to Heston, lifted Heston, Hull–White, and BNS models, and performs favorably in accuracy, efficiency, reliability, and ease of implementation.
